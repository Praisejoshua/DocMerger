    Good to know about merging

    Text, headings, tables, images, lists and most formatting are carried over. Each document starts on a new page and keeps its own page size and margins. Some advanced Word features are not fully preserved:

    • Headers and footers of the 2nd document onwards (they inherit from the first document).
    • Footnotes, endnotes, comments and tracked-change history details.
    • Charts, SmartArt, embedded objects, macros and complex document relationships.
    • If documents use the same style name with different formatting, the first document's version wins.
    • Tables of contents and cross-references may need updating in Word (select all, press F9).
